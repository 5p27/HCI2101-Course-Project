Course Specification

Course Title: Data Visualization Design Course Code: HCI 3401

A. General Information About the Course

1. Credit Hours: 4 Hours (3 Lecture + 2 Lab)

2. Course Type: Required (Department Requirement)

3. Level/Year: 3rd Year / 5th Level

4. Pre-requirements: User Research (HCI 2203) and Database (DS 2201)

5. Co-requirements: None


Course Description:

This course focuses on the principles and techniques of designing effective data visualizations within the context of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). It covers visual perception, information theory, storytelling with data, and creating user-centered visual interfaces.
