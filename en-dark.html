Course Specification

Course Title: Data Visualization Design Course Code: HCI 3401

A. General Information About the Course

1. Credit Hours: 4 Hours

2. Course Type: Required (Department)

3. Level/Year: 3rd Year / 5th Level

4. Pre-requirements: User Research (HCI 2203) & Database (DS 2201)

Course General Description:

This course focuses on the principles and techniques of designing effective data visualizations within the context of HCI. It covers visual perception, storytelling with data, and interactive interfaces.
