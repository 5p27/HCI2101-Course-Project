Course Specification: Data Visualization Design (HCI 3401)

A. General Information

1. Course Title: Data Visualization Design

2. Course Code: HCI 3401

3. Credit Hours: 4 Hours (3 Lecture + 2 Lab)

4. Level/Year: 3rd Year / 5th Level

5. Department: Software Engineering

6. Pre-requirements: User Research (HCI 2203) and Database (DS 2201)


Course General Description:

This course focuses on the principles and techniques of designing effective data visualizations within the context of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). It covers visual perception, storytelling with data, and creating user-centered visual interfaces for complex datasets.
