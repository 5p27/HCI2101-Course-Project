Course Specification

Course Title: Data Visualization Design | Course Code: HCI 3401

A. General Information About the Course

1. Credit Hours: (4)

2. Course Type:

University College Department Track

Required Elective

3. Level/Year: 3rd year/ 5th level

4. Course General Description: This course focuses on the principles and techniques of designing effective data visualizations within the context of HCI. Students will learn how to represent complex data in intuitive ways.

5. Pre-requirements: HCI2203 - User Research, DS2201 - Introduction to Database

6. Co-requisites: None


7. Course Main Objectives:

  • Understand foundational principles of visual perception.
  • Design user-centered, intuitive visualizations.
  • Utilize modern tools for interactive representations.

8. Contact Hours:

Lectures: 30 | Laboratory: 60 | Total: 90
