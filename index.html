Course Title: Data Visualization Design | Code: HCI 3401

A. General Information

1. Credit Hours: 4 Hours [cite: 116]

2. Level/Year: 3rd Year / 5th Level [cite: 132]

3. Pre-requirements: User Research (HCI2203), Introduction to Database (DS2201) [cite: 138, 139]


Course Description

This course focuses on the principles and techniques of designing effective data visualizations within the context of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). [cite: 134] Topics include visual perception, storytelling with data, and creating user-centered visual interfaces. [cite: 136]
