Course Description [cite: 133]

This course focuses on the principles and techniques of designing effective data visualizations within the context of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI). [cite: 134] Students will learn how to represent complex data in intuitive and interactive ways, leveraging design principles, cognitive psychology, and visualization tools. [cite: 135]

Main Objective [cite: 142]

Understand and apply foundational principles of data visualization, including visual perception and cognitive psychology. [cite: 144]
